Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • Plain-Language Draft for Community Review

Privacy-First Commitment: We never sell your personal data or share private student contact details publicly.
1. Information We Collect

When you create an account on Pallotti BookLoop, we collect your name, college email, department, academic year, and optional phone/WhatsApp number. We also record textbook listings and exchange history.

2. Privacy-Conscious Contact Sharing

Your email address and phone number are never displayed publicly on book listings or in search results. Contact information is only shared between two students after an exchange request has been officially Accepted by the book owner.

3. Passwords & Security

Passwords are encrypted using industry-standard Werkzeug salted hashes. We never store plaintext passwords. Sessions utilize secure, HttpOnly cookies with CSRF tokens protecting all state-changing interactions.

4. External Storage & APIs

Book cover images may be stored on external secure cloud storage providers (such as Cloudinary). ISBN queries connect anonymously to the Open Library public API without transmitting student identity information.

5. Account Deletion

Students can request account deactivation or removal of their listings at any time by contacting support or through an administrator.
