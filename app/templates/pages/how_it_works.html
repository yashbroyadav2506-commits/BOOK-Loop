Step-by-Step Guide

How Pallotti BookLoop Works

A clear, transparent guide to listing, requesting, and exchanging academic books safely on campus.

1

Create & Verify Your Student Account

Register with your name, college email, department, and academic year. Click the secure verification link sent to your inbox to unlock book listing and request privileges.

2

List Books You've Completed

Use the List a Book form to input your textbook's title, condition, and course semester. Enter an ISBN to autofill book details and cover images from Open Library in seconds, or upload a photo directly.

3

Discover & Submit Exchange Requests

Browse the Marketplace by department or semester. When you find a book you need, click Request Exchange. You can attach a personal message and optionally offer one of your own books in return.

4

Meet on Campus & Complete the Handover

When the book owner accepts your request, the book is automatically marked Reserved, preventing conflicts. Contact details and preferred campus meeting spots (e.g. Central Library) are unlocked for both students. Meet up, hand over the book, and mark the exchange Completed!
