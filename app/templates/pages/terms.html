Terms of Use

Last Updated: October 2026 • Plain-Language Draft for Community Review

Note: This is a plain-language draft of community guidelines and should be reviewed by college coordinators before formal campus launch.
1. Community Eligibility

Pallotti BookLoop is designed for students, faculty, and alumni of St. Vincent Pallotti College of Engineering & Technology. Users must provide accurate profile information and respect the academic integrity of the community.

2. Permitted Book Listings

Only academic textbooks, curriculum references, lab manuals, and competitive exam preparation books (e.g. GATE, GRE, CAT) may be listed. Commercial advertising, pirated software, counterfeit copies, or unrelated merchandise are strictly prohibited.

3. Exchange Commitments

When a student accepts an exchange request, the book is reserved. Students are expected to communicate promptly and complete the handover at designated campus locations. If an exchange cannot be completed, participants should promptly cancel or mark the request appropriately.

4. Moderation & Termination

Administrators and student moderators reserve the right to remove listings, dismiss requests, or deactivate accounts that violate community rules, post misleading information, or fail to follow through on exchanges.

5. Non-Endorsement Disclaimer

Pallotti BookLoop is an independent student initiative created to facilitate book reuse. Unless explicitly stated, it is not an official administrative wing of SVPCET.
