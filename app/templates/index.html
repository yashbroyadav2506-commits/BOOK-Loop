Simple 3-Step Process

How Pallotti BookLoop Works

Designed specifically for the St. Vincent Pallotti campus community.

1. List Your Academic Books

Enter title, author, and condition. Use our instant ISBN lookup tool to fetch details automatically in seconds.

2. Discover & Request

Filter by engineering department, year, or subject. Request an exchange and optionally offer one of your own books.

3. Meet on Campus & Exchange

Once accepted, the book is reserved. Connect via student contact, meet in the library or department, and mark completed!

Why Exchange Books?

Empowering SVPCET Students

Make Education More Affordable

Save thousands of rupees each academic year by borrowing or exchanging semester textbooks rather than purchasing brand new copies.

Promote Reuse & Sustainability

Give textbooks a circular lifespan. A single textbook can pass through multiple batches of engineering students across years.

Senior-Junior Academic Mentorship

Book exchanges naturally create touchpoints between senior and junior students for sharing notes, syllabus tips, and course advice.
